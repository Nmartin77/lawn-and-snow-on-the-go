Lawn and Snow on the Go

Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the Lawn and Snow on the Go customer app and crew app collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. The apps are operated by (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, and for crews, your company name. Your password is stored in scrambled form by our sign-in provider and cannot be read by us. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Service details: the service you request, your service address, area size, preferred date and notes, plus map coordinates looked up from your address.
  • Quotes and bookings: quotes and messages, booking status and your subscription plan.
  • Payments: payments and crew payouts are handled by Stripe. We keep Stripe account IDs and payout status. We never see or store your full card number or bank account number.
  • Crew location: only if a crew turns on “Share my live location”.
  • Agreement records: the name you typed, the date, and the version of the liability release you signed.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser or device details in server logs, and browser storage that keeps you signed in.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show your request to crews, show quotes to customers, and connect you once a job is booked.
  • To process payments, collect our 15% platform fee, and bill subscriptions.
  • To secure accounts, including optional two-step verification, and to send account emails such as confirmations and password reset links.
  • To follow the law, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Crews can see your request, including the service address, so they can give an accurate quote.
  • Your name and phone number are shared only with the crew you book.
  • Customers see a crew’s company name, quotes and messages. After booking, a customer can see that crew’s live location if the crew has chosen to share it.

Companies that help us

We use these providers to operate the apps. They handle your information only to provide their services, under their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server code.
  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions, card storage and crew payouts. Crews give Stripe identity and bank details directly.
  • Google: maps, address lookup and optional Google sign-in.
  • Apple: optional Apple sign-in.
  • GitHub: hosts the website files.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect people’s safety, or to enforce our agreements. If the business is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner.

Location

Crew location sharing is optional and off by default. When a crew turns it off, the saved position is deleted. We do not track customer locations beyond the service address you enter.

Browser storage and cookies

We use browser storage to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or our own third-party trackers. Google Maps and Stripe may set their own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account. We may keep payment and tax records and records needed to resolve disputes for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update your contact details and address in the customer app’s Account page. To get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account, contact us below. Depending on where you live, such as California, you may have legal rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information. We will not treat you differently for using those rights. To avoid sharing data with Google or Apple, sign in with email instead.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and access rules limit which records each account can see. You can add two-step verification under Security. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

The apps are for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your state privacy rights

If you live in a state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to get a copy of it; to correct it; to delete it; and to opt out of the sale of your information, sharing it for targeted advertising, and profiling. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not profile you in ways that have legal or similarly significant effects.

In the past 12 months we collected these categories of information: identifiers (name, email, phone number, IP address); customer records (service address); commercial information (requests, quotes, bookings, subscriptions); geolocation (service address coordinates, and crew live location when shared); internet activity (server logs); and records you give us (your signed agreement). We disclosed them only to the companies listed above, for the business purposes described in this policy.

To make a request, email us at the address below. We may ask you to confirm the email address on your account so we can verify it is you. You may use an authorized agent. We will respond within the time the law requires. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and you may contact your state attorney general.

Where information is processed

Our providers may process your information in the United States and in other countries where they operate.

Do Not Track

The apps do not track you across other websites, so they do not respond to Do Not Track signals.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
